04 / Useful details

Questions, answered
without the gloss.

Does every phone work with an NFC card?+

No. The device must support NFC and have it available. Compatibility and how the prompt appears can vary by device and settings.

Will a tap make someone leave a review?+

No. A tap opens the configured destination. Customers decide whether to leave a review and what to say. ProtoxTap does not guarantee reviews, ratings or other outcomes.

Is the Insights dashboard available now?+

No. It is planned and is not available until it has been implemented and tested. System tap recording is part of the service but does not mean a customer-facing dashboard is available.

Is the Complete website available now?+

No. The one-page website is planned and not available until it is ready to deliver. The design-change allowance applies only once the included website service is operational; unused requests do not roll over.

How many cards and businesses does a plan cover?+

Up to 3 cards per subscription for one physical business. A subscription does not automatically cover multiple separate businesses or provide 3 cards per location.

How do I buy or cancel a plan?+

This page does not take payment. Contact ProtoxTap by email to enquire. The operator must confirm the purchasing, billing and cancellation process before subscriptions are accepted; see the Cancellation and Refund Policy.
